Frequently Asked

How do I track my order? expand_more
Once your order has shipped, you will receive an email containing tracking information. You can also track your order status in your Karavya account under "Order History".
What is your return policy? expand_more
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original condition with all tags attached. Please initiate the return process through our concierge portal.
Do you offer alterations? expand_more
Complimentary basic alterations are available for full-price items purchased in our boutiques. For online purchases, please contact our concierge to find an authorized tailor near you.
